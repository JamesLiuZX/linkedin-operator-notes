# 90-day credibility sprint

**North star:** Become known as the PM who explains how prediction markets and AI products actually get built.

**Cadence:** 2 posts/week (weeks 1–4) → 2–3 posts/week (weeks 5–12) + daily 10-min engagement.

**Today's date context:** start whenever you begin — treat "Week 1" as your first publishing week.

---

## Phase 1 — Habit (Days 1–30)

**Goal:** Publish 8 posts. Kill the identity conflict. Profile looks coherent.

| Week | Mon / Tue | Thu / Fri | Extra |
|------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Profile update + Post 01 (post-event retention) | Post 02 (PM opens PRs) | Comment 10 min/day |
| 2 | Post 03 (rewards vs farming) | Post 04 (AI partnership lessons) | Save 10 ideas to idea bank |
| 3 | Post 05 (compliance as product) | Post 06 (gen media product lens) | First "thoughtful DM" to someone who engaged |
| 4 | Post 07 (AI trading agent prototype) | Post 08 (three trust surfaces) | Review analytics; pick best post to Featured |

**Phase 1 exit criteria**
- [ ] Profile rewritten
- [ ] 8 posts live
- [ ] Commenting habit ≥5 days/week
- [ ] Cringe feels boring, not terrifying

---

## Phase 2 — Signal (Days 31–60)

**Goal:** Repeatable system + stronger formats. Start collecting email interest.

| Week | Content mix | System |
|------|-------------|--------|
| 5 | 1 teardown + 1 tradeoff post | Create Notion/Sheets Content OS (see `04-content-os.md`) |
| 6 | 1 build log + 1 markets note | Draft newsletter welcome email |
| 7 | 1 carousel OR long teardown + 1 short post | Soft CTA: "I turn these into weekly notes — comment NOTES" |
| 8 | 2 posts + 1 "myths about prediction markets" | Pick Substack/Beehiiv; reserve name |

**Phase 2 exit criteria**
- [ ] 16+ total posts
- [ ] Content OS used weekly
- [ ] Newsletter name chosen, landing page draft exists
- [ ] At least 5 quality DMs exchanged

---

## Phase 3 — Leverage (Days 61–90)

**Goal:** Own an owned channel (email). Package compounds.

| Week | Content | Product move |
|------|---------|--------------|
| 9 | 2 LinkedIn posts | Launch newsletter issue #1 (bundle best atoms) |
| 10 | 2 posts + cross-post newsletter | Issue #2; pin signup in Featured |
| 11 | 2 posts | Publish free compound: "Prediction Market Product Checklist" (PDF or Notion) |
| 12 | 2 posts + retrospective | Outline optional cohort: "Prediction Market Product Design" (don't sell yet — validate interest) |

**Phase 3 exit criteria**
- [ ] Newsletter live with ≥4 issues planned / ≥2 shipped
- [ ] One lead magnet published
- [ ] Clear inbound: intros, coffee chats, or opportunity signals
- [ ] Decide: continue free-only vs design a paid workshop for Q+1

---

## Monthly review (30 min, last Sunday)

Answer in a note:

1. Which pillar got the best *quality* engagement (not likes — saves, DMs, follows from target people)?
2. Which post felt least cringe and most true?
3. What confidential line did I almost cross? (tighten rules)
4. What opportunity showed up?
5. Next month: double down on which 2 formats?
