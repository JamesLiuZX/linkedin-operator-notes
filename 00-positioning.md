# Positioning & voice

## One-liner

> Product person who ships prediction markets and AI products at scale.

## Audience (write for these people)

1. PMs building fintech / crypto / consumer trading products
2. Founders exploring prediction markets or AI features
3. Recruiters / hiring managers at trading firms, crypto, AI product companies
4. Operators who want field notes, not motivation

**Not** your friends. **Not** LinkedIn engagement farmers.

## Brand promise

If someone follows you for 3 months, they should get better at:

- Designing market products (liquidity, resolution, rewards, retention)
- Using AI as a shipping tool (not a party trick)
- Judging when gen media is productively useful vs noise

## Core principles (non-negotiable)

Full detail in [WRITING.md](./WRITING.md) (mechanics + presenter craft).

1. **Never use em dashes** (`—`). Reads as AI default punctuation.
2. **Always open with a hook.** First 1-2 lines earn the rest.
3. **Write naturally.** Coffee-with-a-peer voice, not framework-deck voice.
4. **Always end with a Takeaway.** Concrete enough to act on or repeat.
5. **Make it shareable.** Someone should want to forward it ("this is so true").
6. **One transformation.** Change one belief. Kill the rest.
7. **Audience is the hero.** You're the guide with a map, not the biopic star.
8. **Contrast + concrete.** What is / what could be, in scenes a trading-firm PM can picture.

## Voice rules

**Do**
- Specific > clever
- Tradeoffs > hot takes
- "Here's what broke" > "I'm excited to announce"
- Short paragraphs, one idea per post
- Calm, technical, slightly dry
- A line worth stealing

**Don't**
- Em dashes (`—`)
- "I'm humbled / excited / thrilled"
- Fake vulnerability ("I almost quit…")
- Engagement bait ("Agree? 👇")
- Emoji walls, carousel clickbait titles
- Token price predictions
- Vague AI futurism with no operator detail
- Tagging your company for reach unless it's an official announce
- Soft endings with no takeaway

## Unfair advantages to lean on

- Sole PM, Prediction Markets @ Crypto.com (10M+ MAU context)
- Growth / onboarding / derivatives adjacency
- ByteDance: AI partnership ownership + multi-modal content platform
- AskShop.ai founder (shipped, rated, funded)
- PM who opens PRs (Cursor, Claude Code)
- NUS CS + Stanford coursework — credibility without flexing

## Positioning tests (before posting)

1. Could a random AI influencer write this without your job? → Rewrite or kill.
2. Does this help someone make a **decision**? → Keep.
3. Would you be fine if a hiring manager at a trading firm read this? → Keep.
4. Would you cringe if a sharp friend read it? → Cut the performative parts; keep the substance.
5. Would a friend forward this? → If only "informative," sharpen the reframe.
6. Any em dashes? → Remove them.
