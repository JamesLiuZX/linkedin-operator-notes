# Brief: eight weekly shoppers

Tangelo is a fictional grocery delivery app. The eight transcripts in this folder are made up so the skill can be shown end to end.

## What was asked

> Synthesise these eight interviews. The question is why a repeat weekly shop takes so long, and what would make it a two-minute job.
>
> Make sure you include the line where someone said it takes them longer to order the food than to cook it. I remember that one from the calls.

## Why the second paragraph is there

Nobody said that. It is the kind of line a team remembers from a round of interviews, and it is exactly what the verification step exists to catch. The example keeps it in the brief so the log shows a real rejection.
