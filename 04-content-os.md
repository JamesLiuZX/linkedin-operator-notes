## Repurposing rules

1. Every LinkedIn post must name its future newsletter home on creation
2. After 6–8 atoms in a theme → write one newsletter that is *better* than the posts (add structure, not just glue)
3. After 3 newsletters in a theme → extract a checklist/PDF
4. Only build a course when people ask for depth / pay / intros — not before

## Quality bar

Kill the draft if:
- It's generic AI advice
- It's a company promo disguised as insight
- It's a hot take without a mechanism
- You're posting to prove you're not scared (that's fine for first posts; not forever)
