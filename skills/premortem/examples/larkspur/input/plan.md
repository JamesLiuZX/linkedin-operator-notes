# Plan: team suggestions

Larkspur is a fictional planning tool for product and engineering teams. This plan and everything around it is made up so the skill can be shown end to end.

## What ships

After signup, Larkspur asks the trial admin for access to their work calendar. It finds the five people the admin meets most often and shows them in one card: "Invite all 5". One button sends all five an invite. The five join as free guests for the length of the 14-day trial.

## Who gets it

Every new trial workspace. Planned for February 2027.

## What it is meant to change

- More trial workspaces have a second active person in the first week.
- More trials pay.

## What we already know

- **Journey teardown (code T):** four products, three stages, 24 screens. Two competitors ask for teammates during signup, and Larkspur does not. One competitor finds colleagues in a chat tool and lists them with nobody ticked. One lets any page be shared by a link, and people who only look are free. Larkspur puts "Invite your team" last on an empty Home, with no button. Its invite sits under Settings, then Members. Its Add member dialog says "Adding a member starts billing today: $12 per month", after a signup page that says "Free for 14 days. No card needed." There is no view-only option and no preview of what the new person will see.
- **Interview synthesis (code S):** eight people who started a trial alone. Six of eight held the workspace back until it looked presentable. Five of eight wanted someone to look at the plan, not edit it. Five of eight could not tell what an invite costs or when billing starts. Four of eight did not know what an invitee would see. Three of eight sent an invite email that was never opened. Three of eight work where a new tool needs a security or IT review. One of the eight added the whole team within twenty minutes of signing up.
- There is no data on calendar-based suggestions in this plan. No baseline has been measured.

## What we can measure today

- Workspaces with a second active person within 7 days
- Invites sent and accepted
- Trials that convert to paid within 30 days
- Workspaces that report an unwanted invite
- Invite emails marked as spam
- Contacts to support, by reason
- Paid seats per paying workspace

## Constraints

- The security team has not reviewed calendar access.
- Calendar access needs approval from each calendar provider. That review has not started.
- Billing for a guest who becomes a member is not designed.
