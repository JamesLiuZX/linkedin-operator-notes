# Post 08 — Three trust surfaces

**Status:** ready to edit

## Draft

A prediction market product has three trust surfaces.

Most teams obsess over one and leak the other two.

**1) Liquidity / price quality**  
If spreads are garbage or markets feel dead, users learn the product is a toy. Fancy UI won't save it.

**2) Resolution clarity**  
People can accept losing a bet.  
They cannot accept not understanding how the outcome was decided.  
Ambiguous rules are not a legal detail — they are a UX defect with financial consequences.

**3) Product UX integrity**  
Onboarding, fees, position display, latency, notifications after resolve — every friction says "this venue might surprise you." In markets, surprise is expensive.

You can grow with campaigns.  
You cannot grow past broken trust surfaces.

When I evaluate a markets roadmap item, I ask:

Does this improve liquidity quality, resolution clarity, or UX integrity?  
If no — is it just decoration during a spike?

Prediction markets aren't hard because odds are hard.

They're hard because trust is a multi-system property — trading, ops, compliance, and design all share the blast radius.

Build for the user who lost money fairly and still came back.  
That user is your real retention cohort.
