## Draft

Every point in this PRD has a number. The number goes everywhere.

Point 2.1 is 2.1 in the priority table, in the rollout plan and on the prototype's switch. A reviewer can say yes to 1.1 and not yet to 2.2, and everyone knows what that means.

Each point is four things:
· the problem, as the user meets it today
· the change, as a move on the screen
· the evidence beside it: a screen, a count or a risk
· how we would know: what to measure first, what should move, what means stop

A script checks 6 things before the page reaches you. Every point has a problem, a change and evidence. Every ID in the tables exists as a point. A title that opens with a verb, such as Add or Build, is rejected, so it gets rewritten as what the user faces. Every figure has a source or is marked as an estimate.

That last check caught my own first draft on 18 November 2026. It held a figure with no label on it.

The part I owe: the grocery example was written alongside the skill, not produced by a fresh agent session. On 19 November 2026 I ran the skill fresh on 2 other invented companies. Both passed 6 of 6 checks. Both also got a number past that last check without a label, one by moving it to another field. The checker warns on that now, and a pass on it says less than it looks.

The example is for an invented grocery app: 6 points in 3 sections. 3 are in the first release, 2 wait on other teams, and 1 waits for data.

Takeaway: number every point once, and make every later document use the same number.
