# Engagement playbook

Posting without commenting is broadcasting into a void. 10 minutes/day compounds.

## Who to engage with (build a list of ~30)

**Categories**
- Prediction market builders (Polymarket-adjacent PMs, Kalshi commentary, academic market design)
- Crypto product / trading PMs (exchanges, perps, options — peer level)
- AI product builders who ship (not prompt influencers)
- Fintech / growth PMs with strong writing
- A few sharp journalists / researchers in markets

**How to find**
- People who wrote something specific you learned from
- Commenters on good posts in your niche
- Speakers from conferences you respect

Save them in a note: `Name | why | LinkedIn URL`

## Comment formula (not "Great post!")

1. Agree or disagree with **one specific line**
2. Add a mechanism, counterexample, or question
3. Stop. No self-promo link.

**Example**
> The retention point after events is underrated. We've seen spikes that look like product-market fit until the calendar goes quiet — the real design problem is the habit loop in week two, not the landing page for the final.

## DM rules

**Do DM when**
- Someone left a thoughtful comment on your post
- You left 2+ good comments on theirs and there's a natural thread
- You can offer a concrete observation (not "can I pick your brain?")

**DM template**
> Thanks for the note on [specific thing]. I've been writing operator notes on [markets / AI shipping] — your point on [X] matches what I'm seeing around [Y]. Curious how you think about [one sharp question].

**Don't**
- Pitch your newsletter in DM #1
- Ask for a job in DM #1
- Send a LinkedIn connection note that's a resume

## Connection requests

- Personalized one-liner referencing their work
- Cap ~10–15/week early on — quality over spray
- When people connect after your post: accept + short thanks if they commented

## What "good engagement" looks like after 30 days

- Same 10–20 names recognizing you
- Occasional inbound from people you'd actually want to talk to
- Comments that start debates with substance
