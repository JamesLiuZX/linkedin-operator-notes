# The request

What the agent was sent, word for word. The test, the company and the numbers are fictional.

> We ran the share by link test on new trial workspaces, with free viewers. 50/50 split, planned for 21 days, and it ran the full 21 days, 7 to 27 September 2026. Hypothesis: letting the trial admin share a page by link, with viewers who are free, gets more workspaces a second active person within 7 days. Conversion to paid is counted 30 days after each trial starts, and the counts were pulled on 28 October 2026, so every workspace had its full 30 days.
>
> Control: 9,836 workspaces, 2,164 of them had a second active person within 7 days.
> Share by link: 9,902 workspaces, 2,575 had a second active person within 7 days.
>
> Before the test we agreed the smallest lift worth shipping is 2 points.
>
> Guardrails, all counted per workspace, so the bases are the workspaces (9,836 control, 9,902 share by link):
>
> - Trials that convert to paid within 30 days: 1,082 vs 1,099. A fall is the harm. We would accept a fall of at most 1 point.
> - Workspaces that reported an unwanted invite: 59 vs 64. A rise is the harm. Tolerance 0.5 points.
> - Workspaces that turned sharing off: 118 vs 131. A rise is the harm. Tolerance 0.5 points.
>
> Should we ship?
