Activation

ω0 scales the first layer only, which is what sets how fast the field varies across space. Weight gain scales the hidden layers. Near 1.00× you get broad organic forms; push it up and each layer beats against the one below until the field breaks into interference and then into noise. Sharing one large frequency across every layer is what makes deep sine networks unstable, and you can watch that happen here.
