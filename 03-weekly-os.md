# Weekly operating system

Total time target: **~2.5–3.5 hours/week**. Protect this like a meeting.

---

## Sunday — Batch (60–90 min)

1. Pick 2 prompts from `ideas/idea-bank.md` or unfinished drafts in `posts/`
2. Write both drafts in one sitting (ugly first drafts OK)
3. Run the positioning tests from `00-positioning.md`
4. Compliance pass: strip anything confidential
5. Schedule or put in Notes app with publish dates
6. Add 3 new ideas to the bank from the week’s work

## Daily — Engage (10 min, weekday)

See `05-engagement-playbook.md`. Non-negotiable. Distribution > perfection.

## Publish days — Ship (15 min)

1. Post
2. First 30–60 min: reply to every serious comment
3. Do **not** refresh likes. Close the app.

## Friday — Capture (15 min)

Dump into idea bank:
- Decision you made this week
- Bug / edge case in markets or AI tooling
- Question a stakeholder asked that others would learn from
- Something that failed

---

## Batching template (copy per draft)

```
Title (internal):
Pillar: Markets / AI ships / Gen media
Format: teardown | tradeoff | build log | myth | lesson
Hook (first 2 lines):
Body:
CTA (optional, soft):
Public-safe? Y/N
Reuse later as: newsletter section ___ / course module ___
```

---

## Energy rules (anti-burnout)

- Never write when trying to "sound impressive"
- If stuck 15 min → switch prompt; don't force
- Missed a day of commenting → fine. Missed a week of posting → publish a short tradeoff post same day, don't "catch up" with 4 posts
- One week off per quarter is fine; announce nothing
