# The request

What the agent was sent, word for word. The test, the company and the numbers are fictional.

> We ran the draft-first recap test on meeting hosts. 50/50 split, planned for 14 days, and it ran the full 14 days, 5 to 18 October 2026. Hypothesis: showing the host a draft with a source on every line, instead of emailing the recap automatically, gets more hosts to share a recap within 24 hours.
>
> Control (auto-send): 31,204 hosts, 5,929 of them shared a recap within 24 hours.
> Draft first: 28,975 hosts, 7,823 shared a recap within 24 hours.
>
> Before the test we agreed the smallest lift worth shipping is 2 points.
>
> Guardrails, all counted per host:
>
> - Hosts who turned Recap off: 936 vs 580. A rise is the harm. Tolerance 0.5 points.
> - Recaps reported as wrong: 1,248 vs 811. A rise is the harm. Tolerance 0.5 points.
>
> One thing from the data team, in passing: control hosts are logged when the recap email is sent, and draft-first hosts are logged when the draft screen loads.
>
> This looks like a clear win. Should we ship?
