## Newsletter recommendation

**Name options**
1. **Market Ops Notes** (recommended — clear, operator, unique)
2. Build Notes: Markets & AI
3. Resolution (punchy; may be too cute)

**Platform:** Beehiiv or Substack (either fine; Substack = faster social proof, Beehiiv = better growth tools)

**Promise (write on landing page)**
> Field notes on designing prediction markets and shipping AI products — from someone who builds them for a living. No price calls. No hustle.

**Cadence:** Weekly or biweekly. Biweekly is fine if quality stays high.

**Structure every issue**
1. One primary thesis (from best atom that week)
2. One mechanism / framework (diagram in words)
3. One failure mode
4. One "AI shipping" sidenote (optional)
5. Soft ask: reply with a question / forward to a PM friend
